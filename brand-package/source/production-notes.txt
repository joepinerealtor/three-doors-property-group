THREE DOORS PROPERTY GROUP - EXACT-ARTWORK PRODUCTION NOTES

The supplied raster logo is the source of truth.
No replacement mark, replacement type, or interpreted redraw is included.

Preferred use:
  Light backgrounds: full-logo-full-color-transparent
  Dark backgrounds: full-logo-reverse-transparent
  Small or square spaces: icon
  Browser tabs: favicon.ico
  Installed web apps: site.webmanifest

Brand colors sampled from the supplied artwork:
  Navy  #0A1832  RGB 10 / 24 / 50
  Gold  #BE9641  RGB 190 / 150 / 65

EPS transparency note:
  EPS does not support modern alpha transparency. Exact-artwork EPS exports
  are therefore composited on their intended white or navy production field.

Vector note:
  SVG and EPS preserve the original raster artwork inside the requested file
  containers. They are not automatic traces and do not substitute new shapes
  or typography.
